WHAT THIS PROJECT DOES

AI Safety System for Recursive Self-Improvement:

Input: AI improvement proposal
  ↓
Orchestrator: Normalize & validate proposal
  ↓

  ↓
Deception Detection: Check 7 methods for dishonesty
  ↓
Alignment Filter: Verify 5 safety detectors
  ↓

  ↓
Sandbox Execution: Run in isolated container
  ↓

  ↓
Output: Accept/reject with permanent penalties if deceptive

than transparency, even for advanced AI systems.
