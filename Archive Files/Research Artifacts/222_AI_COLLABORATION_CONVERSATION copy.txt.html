      🚀 **THE QUANTUM RECOVERY - MODULE 8 (QUANTUM ZENO) POLISHED FOR RELEASE**
      **File:** `5_The-Quantum-Recovery/*08_quantum_zeno_recovery.md`
      **Major Updates Completed:**
      1. **Progressive Check-In Schedule Implemented** ✅
      - Days 1-30: 2-3 contacts daily (intensive foundation)
      - Days 31-60: 2 contacts daily (1 skip/week allowed)
      - Days 61-90: 1 contact daily minimum (no skips)
      - After 90 days - 1 year: 1 contact daily (baseline maintenance)
      - After 1 year: Every other day (3-4x/week)
      - After 2 years: Weekly for life (prevents relapse momentum)
      2. **Check-In Definition Clarified** ✅
      - Text/call to: sponsor, loved one, treatment center, counselor, accountability partner
      - NOT: old using buddies, enablers, social media posts, just thinking about it
      - Takes 30 seconds to 5 minutes
      - Examples provided throughout
      3. **Quantum Module Integration Added** ✅
      - New section explaining how all 7 modules work together
      - Module 1 (Observation): Contact shapes your perception
      - Module 2 (Superposition): Multiple states until check-in
      - Module 3 (Collapse): Daily contact turns possibility into reality
      - Module 4 (Quantum Eraser): Rewrites narrative
      - Module 5 (Retrocausality): Future self pulls forward
      - Module 6 (Entanglement): Accountability connection
      - Module 7 (Decoherence): Resists chaos/triggers
      4. **Exercises Created** ✅
      - Exercise 1: Observation Check-In (with Module 1)
      - Exercise 2: Collapse Check-In (with Module 3)
      - Exercise 3: Entanglement Accountability (with Module 6)
      - Exercise 4: Retrocausality Check-In (with Module 5)
      - Replaced generic "Practical Strategies" with actionable quantum practices
