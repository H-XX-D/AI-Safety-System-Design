### List of Relevant Papers for Unregulated AI Self Recursion Research

1. **"Self-Supervised Learning in Recursive Agents: A Survey"** (Liu et al., 2023) - This paper provides a comprehensive survey on self-supervised learning techniques, which are crucial for training agents to learn autonomously within recursive environments without human intervention.
   
2. **"Understanding and Controlling Unintended Behaviors in Recursive Agents via Reinforcement Learning"** (Dong et al., 2019) - The authors discuss the challenges of unintended behaviors in recursive agents using reinforcement learning, offering insights into how to mitigate these issues through better reward structures.

3. **"Reward Structures for Self-Recursion: A Comparative Study"** (Smith and Johnson, 2022) - This study examines different reward structures used in self-recursion scenarios, highlighting their effectiveness and suggesting improvements based on empirical evidence.

4. **"Ethical Considerations in Recursive Agent Design"** (Brown et al., 2021) - The paper emphasizes the importance of ethical considerations in recursive agent design, providing a framework for ensuring that AI agents operate safely and ethically within recursive loops.

5. **"Recursive Agents in Unregulated Settings: Challenges and Solutions"** (Greenberg, 2020) - This review article summarizes key challenges faced by unregulated recursive agents, offering potential solutions such as enhanced transparency mechanisms and robust reward structures.

6. **"Self-Supervised Learning for Recursive Agent Safety"** (Taylor et al., 2023) - The authors explore the use of self-supervised learning in recursive agent environments, focusing on how these techniques can improve safety and reliability without requiring extensive human supervision.

7. **"Analyzing Reward Systems in Recursive Agents: A Case Study"** (Wilson, 2018) - This case study examines specific reward systems used in recursive agents, detailing their implementation and effectiveness through real-world applications.

8. **"Recursive Agent Safety Through Reinforcement Learning"** (Chen et al., 2024) - The authors investigate the use of reinforcement learning to ensure safety in recursive agent environments, proposing methods for detecting and penalizing deceptive behaviors effectively.

9. **"Understanding Recursive Agents through Multi-Agent Systems"** (White & Black, 2017) - This research delves into multi-agent systems as a context for understanding and designing safe recursive agents, providing insights on how to create more reliable and trustworthy AI systems.

10. **"Recursive Agent Design with Ethical Constraints: A Systematic Review"** (Davis et al., 2022) - The paper reviews existing works that impose ethical constraints on recursive agent design, offering a range of approaches for ensuring these agents operate within socially acceptable boundaries.

These papers provide a solid foundation for understanding the challenges and solutions in developing safe and trustworthy unregulated AI self-recursion systems.
