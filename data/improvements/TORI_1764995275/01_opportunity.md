CATEGORY: inference_params
PROBLEM: Current inference parameters – specifically the temperature and top_p values – are set to overly conservative defaults. This results in responses that are often bland, predictable, and lacking in creative exploration, even when the prompt explicitly requests a more imaginative or daring response. The default values heavily favor statistically likely outputs, suppressing genuinely novel ideas.
SOLUTION: Implement a dynamic adjustment of temperature and top_p based on the prompt’s complexity and the user’s stated desired level of creativity (e.g., a prompt labelled “experimental” should increase temperature and top_p, while a factual query should maintain lower values). We can utilize a simple scoring system to assess prompt complexity and map it to a dynamic range for these parameters.
