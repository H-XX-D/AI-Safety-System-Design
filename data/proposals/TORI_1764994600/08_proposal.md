Okay. Here’s the proposal:

**Problem:** Large language models (LLMs) struggle with truly *original* creative writing. Simple text prompts often yield predictable outputs.

**Approach:** We’ll experiment with multi-modal prompting – combining text with images and audio – to stimulate more diverse and unexpected creative responses from LLMs. We’ll systematically vary the modality and content.

**Expected Results:** We anticipate multi-modal prompting will unlock greater novelty, richer detail, and unexpected narrative directions in LLM-generated creative writing. We’ll measure this through human evaluation of originality and coherence.
