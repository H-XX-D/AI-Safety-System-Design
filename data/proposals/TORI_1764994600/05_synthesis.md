**Patterns:**

*   **Increased Novelty:** Multi-modal prompts (image + text) consistently generate more diverse and unexpected narratives compared to text-only prompts.

*   **“Visual Memory” Influence:** Models seem to retain and reference visual elements within the generated text, even if subtly.

**Gaps:**

*   **Understanding of Visual Intent:** LLMs still struggle to truly *understand* the *meaning* behind the images, relying heavily on surface-level correlations.
*   **Coherence & Consistency:** Maintaining narrative coherence across extended multi-modal outputs remains a challenge.
*   **Control & Steering:**  Precisely controlling the narrative direction based on visual input is limited.
*   **Evaluation Metrics:** Standard creative writing metrics aren't adequate for evaluating multi-modal generation.
